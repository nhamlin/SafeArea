SafeArea.Space - A Private Safe Area or Space to Vent and Release Stress

"If you must speak ill of another, do not speak it. Write it in the sand near the water's edge." - Napoleon Hill

Write your thoughts in the box below. When you are done, press "Send", and what you have written will disappear into the void forever. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. This exists so you can be impulsive and act on your emotions without jeopardizing your relationships with others. Press "Send" and release the stress without fear.
